# Project Management

## Purpose

Store the project-level information required to define, manage, and hand over an industrial B2B website project.

## Store Here

- Project brief and business objectives
- Target markets, languages, industries, and buyer personas
- Product, Solution, and Application priorities
- Website scope, sitemap, milestones, owners, and approvals
- Brand, terminology, claim, certification, and compliance references
- Meeting notes, decision records, risks, dependencies, and change logs

## Rules

- Keep approved facts separate from assumptions and open questions.
- Record major URL, scope, tracking, and publishing decisions.
- Do not store production credentials, secrets, or unnecessary personal data.
- Use dated or versioned files when project decisions may change.
