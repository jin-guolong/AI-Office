H-1716 Series
Web-fed Digital Printing Machine

 

  • 16 Epson I3200-A1 micro piezo printheads in 8-color configuration
  • Complete inline hardware configuration (customizable functions)
  • Industry-leading ±0.1 mm cutting precision
Smart Integrated Design Streamlines High-Efficiency Digital Printing Workflow
Dayuan Web-fed Digital Printing Machine is purpose-built for short-run and quick-turn orders. No plate making required; printing starts immediately after startup. The machine integrates a full suite of intelligent inline post-press processing procedures. Equipped with high-definition Epson printheads, it supports flexible optional inline functional modules and achieves cutting precision up to ±0.1 mm. Delivering one-stop automatic printing production, the machine efficiently accommodates processing requirements for diverse paper sizes.

FAQ
What is the equipment delivery time?

The delivery cycle for standard models is approximately 30 days; customized models take 45 to 60 days, subject to specific requirements. An accurate delivery schedule will be provided after order confirmation.

How long is the equipment warranty period?

The complete machine comes with a 12-month comprehensive warranty. Within the warranty period, free maintenance and spare part replacement will be provided for malfunctions caused by manufacturing defects.

Does the equipment support personalized customization services?

Customization services are available. Customized solutions can be tailored to specific production requirements. Please contact our sales team for exclusive proposals.

What intelligent functions are integrated into the equipment?

It incorporates a full set of automated processes to realize one-stop full printing workflow: automatic web feeding, digital printing, film laminating, tension control, web guiding, slitting, waste trim rewinding, automatic cross cutting and automatic paper stacking.

What main application fields is this printing machine suitable for?

Targeting the mid-range printing market, it fits various mid-range packaging and paper printing scenarios. It is ideal for mid-tier packaging of daily chemicals, food, general gifts and light industrial goods, precisely covering mid-to-high volume mainstream printing categories.

What color configurations does Dayuan Digital Printing Machine support?

The machine adopts a 16-printhead 8-color setup, consisting of four primary CMYK colors (Cyan, Magenta, Yellow, Black) plus four spot colors. Customers can flexibly select spot colors including light cyan, light magenta, light black, extra light black, orange, green, purple and bright red according to printing demands, catering to diverse printing effects and upgrading finished product texture.

What functional advantages does the supporting software system have?

The machine is equipped with the original Fiery software system from EFI, USA. It supports professional color management, PDF preflight and intelligent imposition, ensuring consistent printing color and accurate file output.

What are the core advantages of Dayuan Digital Printing Machine?

All core components excep printheads and circuit boards are independently developed and manufactured in-house. The complete machine is sold as a whole unit with high automation, effectively avoiding incompatibility issues arising from sourcing parts from multiple brands and ensuring more stable operation.
