# FAQ Rules for Industrial B2B English Websites

## 1. Purpose

FAQs must answer real, recurring buyer questions about industrial machinery, electrical equipment, and industrial components. They should reduce evaluation friction, clarify technical or commercial requirements, and guide the buyer to the correct detailed page or next action.

FAQs are not a place to repeat keywords, duplicate the entire page, publish unsupported claims, or hide essential product information.

## 2. Valid Question Sources

Prioritize questions from:

1. Sales inquiries and RFQs
2. Engineering, installation, maintenance, and support records
3. Distributor and customer feedback
4. GSC queries and site-search data
5. Product documentation and approved training materials
6. Recurring comparison or specification questions found during SERP review

Every FAQ set should record its source type. Search demand alone is not enough when the company cannot answer accurately.

## 3. FAQ Types and Placement

| Question type | Best location |
|---|---|
| Company capability, markets, support | Homepage, about, or capability page |
| Product-family selection | Category page |
| Model, specification, option, compatibility | Product detail page |
| Application, integration, project requirement | Solution/application page |
| Case method or relevance | Case study page |
| Narrow explanatory or troubleshooting question | Blog, guide, support page, or FAQ hub |
| Ordering, MOQ, sample, lead-time process | Product, commercial, or contact page when verified |

Keep the answer closest to the page where the question affects the decision. Do not create an FAQ hub that removes important answers from product or solution pages.

## 4. Question Selection Rules

Approve a question when it:

- Reflects a real buyer concern
- Fits the page’s product, solution, application, or buyer stage
- Can be answered accurately with approved evidence
- Adds useful information not already obvious on the page
- Helps selection, validation, implementation, maintenance, or inquiry

Reject or rewrite questions that:

- Exist only as keyword variations
- Invite an unsupported “yes” answer
- Are too broad for a concise and safe response
- Duplicate another question with different wording
- Ask for project-specific engineering conclusions without required inputs
- Expose confidential, legal, safety, or commercial information

## 5. Answer Structure

Use this sequence when appropriate:

1. Direct answer in the first sentence
2. Conditions, variables, or scope
3. Technical explanation or decision criteria
4. Limitation, safety boundary, or verification requirement
5. Link or CTA for deeper information

Answer rules:

- Use professional, natural English
- Keep the answer concise but complete; there is no mandatory word count
- State “it depends” only when the dependent variables are immediately explained
- Use bullets or a small table when several factors must be compared
- Define abbreviations and maintain consistent units
- Link to a full guide rather than compressing a complex procedure into an unsafe short answer
- Do not make the answer dependent on content hidden from users

## 6. Industry-Specific FAQ Requirements

### 6.1 Industrial machinery

Common valid areas:

- Suitable materials, products, or processes
- Capacity, accuracy, output, and configuration factors
- Utilities, footprint, integration, installation, and commissioning
- Changeover, cleaning, training, maintenance, and spare parts
- Safety systems, operating boundaries, and documentation
- Customization, testing, sample trials, and RFQ inputs

Do not answer performance questions without defining material, configuration, test conditions, and operating assumptions.

### 6.2 Electrical equipment

Common valid areas:

- Voltage, current, frequency, power, phase, and system compatibility
- Ratings, protection, coordination, derating, temperature, and altitude
- IP/NEMA, standards, certification scope, and regional requirements
- Wiring, installation, testing, inspection, and maintenance
- Communication protocols, accessories, replacement, and documentation

Do not provide hazardous live-work advice. State when a qualified electrical professional and local-code review are required.

### 6.3 Industrial components

Common valid areas:

- Material, dimension, tolerance, rating, finish, and manufacturing method
- Fit, interface, compatibility, replacement, and interchangeability
- Load, speed, pressure, temperature, medium, corrosion, and wear
- Installation, lubrication, inspection, maintenance, storage, and packaging
- Custom drawings, samples, quality control, testing, and traceability

Do not confirm interchangeability from a name or visual similarity alone.

## 7. Technical Accuracy and Risk Rules

- Verify specifications against the correct model and document revision
- Distinguish nominal, maximum, typical, rated, and tested values
- State applicable units, standards, conditions, and exclusions
- Never infer certification from design similarity or a related product
- Never guarantee compatibility, lifespan, performance, compliance, delivery, or savings
- Separate general information from project-specific engineering approval
- Require qualified review for electrical safety, machine safety, load, pressure, thermal, chemical, or regulatory advice
- Remove or update answers when products, standards, documents, or commercial policies change

## 8. Commercial FAQ Rules

Commercial answers must be accurate and maintainable:

- MOQ: state only verified standard policy and known exceptions
- Lead time: explain that timing depends on configuration, quantity, materials, approval, and current schedule when applicable
- Price: explain required quotation inputs; do not invent ranges
- Samples: state actual availability, conditions, and evaluation process
- Customization: identify supported scope and required drawings/specifications
- Warranty/support: use approved policy language
- Shipping/markets: state verified coverage and documentation capabilities

Avoid generic answers such as “Yes, we can customize anything.”

## 9. Search and Structured Data Rules

- FAQs must primarily serve users, not rich-result eligibility
- Visible questions and answers must match any FAQ structured data exactly
- Mark up only content that users can access on the page
- Do not duplicate the same FAQ block across many pages unless it is genuinely relevant; prefer page-specific answers
- FAQ structured data does not guarantee a Google rich result
- Do not mark promotional statements, product reviews, or user-generated questions as company-authored FAQ content incorrectly
- Maintain one canonical destination for the full answer when several pages reference it

## 10. Internal Linking and CTA Rules

Each answer may link to:

- A product/category page for specifications or options
- A solution/application page for project context
- A guide, manual, datasheet, drawing, or standard-related resource
- A support or inquiry path when project inputs are required

Use descriptive anchors. Do not add a product link to every answer when it is irrelevant.

CTA examples:

- Review Technical Specifications
- Compare Product Options
- Download the Datasheet
- Discuss Your Application
- Submit Your Drawing
- Request Selection Support

## 11. FAQ Maintenance Workflow

1. Collect questions and tag source, product, market, buyer stage, and frequency.
2. Remove duplicates and assign each question to the best page.
3. Verify sources, model scope, standards, and commercial policy.
4. Draft a direct answer with conditions and limitations.
5. Obtain technical or policy approval where required.
6. Add relevant internal link and CTA.
7. Validate visible content and structured data consistency.
8. Test accordion accessibility, keyboard behavior, and mobile display.
9. Record owner or review trigger for time-sensitive answers.
10. Update based on new questions, product revisions, policy changes, and performance data.

## 12. Codex Checklist

- [ ] Questions come from documented buyer, search, sales, or support evidence
- [ ] Each question is assigned to the most relevant page
- [ ] Duplicates and keyword-only variations are removed
- [ ] First sentence answers the question directly
- [ ] Conditions, units, limitations, and required inputs are stated
- [ ] Product model, document revision, standard, and policy are verified
- [ ] Machinery, electrical, or component-specific risks are addressed
- [ ] No compatibility, certification, result, price, or delivery detail is invented
- [ ] Safety-sensitive answers have qualified review
- [ ] Commercial answers use approved policy language
- [ ] Internal links and CTA support the next buyer decision
- [ ] Visible FAQ and structured data match exactly when markup is used
- [ ] Accordion content is accessible and usable on mobile
- [ ] Time-sensitive answers have a maintenance trigger
- [ ] Internal notes and placeholders are absent from published content
