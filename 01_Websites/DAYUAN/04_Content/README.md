# Content

## Purpose

Store customer-facing and working content for industrial B2B website pages and knowledge resources.

## Store Here

- Homepage, Category, Product, Solution, Application, and Case Study copy
- Blog topic clusters, article briefs, drafts, and final content
- FAQ, technical guides, download copy, and CTA/form text
- Source registers, technical-review notes, and approval records
- Content inventories, calendars, and internal-link plans

## Rules

- Use professional English for overseas industrial buyers.
- Verify specifications, standards, certifications, compatibility, and claims.
- Mark missing information internally; never invent technical or customer facts.
- Keep draft, reviewed, approved, and published versions distinguishable.
