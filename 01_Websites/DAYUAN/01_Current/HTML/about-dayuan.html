Smart Future Stems From Simplification

27 years of R&D and production of high and new technologies
Global Market Leader in Pre-press & Post-press Equipment
Zhejiang Dayuan Machinery operates a modern standardized factory with a total land area of over 98,000 square meters and a construction area exceeding 180,000 square meters. Originally founded in 1999, Dayuan has grown into an integrated enterprise encompassing R&D, production, sales, and service.

Dayuan continuously integrates advanced international technologies, equipping its facilities with over 50 high-precision CNC machining centers from globally renowned brands including DMG and OKUMA, as well as Hexagon 3D CMM inspection equipment.

Over the past 27 years, Dayuan has long been dedicated to the R&D of high-end technologies and the manufacturing of complete printing & packaging equipment. Dayuan now has over 450 employees with annual production capacity of 1000 sets.

Printing package system integrated solution supplier
Dayuan has been deeply engaged in the field of printing and packaging equipment, with a main focus on three major categories: hot stamping & die cutting, die cutting, sheet cutting and digital printing. Its product range includes a diverse selection of models such as multi-unit hot stamping & die cutting machine with stripping, heavy duty die cutting machine, and double helix high speed sheet cutter machines. For hot stamping and die cutting products, the company has established a three-tier product matrix — the BHT (Top-Excellence Series), MHK (High-end Intelligent Series), and MHC (Classic Innovative Series) — to meet the demands of diverse markets.
