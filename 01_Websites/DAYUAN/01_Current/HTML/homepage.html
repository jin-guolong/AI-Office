DAYUAN was established in 1999. We specialize in the R&D and production of automatic hot foil stamping machines, die cutting machines, sheet cutting machines and digital printing machines featuring cutting-edge technologies.

About DAYUAN
Intelligent hot stamping, die cutting, sheet cutting and digital printing
Make the best equipment to create value for customers

Hot Foil Stamping & Die Cutting Machine

Supports hot stamping, enabling stamping and die cutting of cardboard and other materials, widely used in post-press packaging.

Flatbed Die Cutting Machine

Applies die pressure to cut and crease cardboard/corrugated board into shape, covering from 820mm-2100mm.

Sheet Cutter Machine

Precisely cuts large paper rolls into uniform sheets for printing, die cutting, and hot stamping. Widely used in packaging and paper.

Web-fed Digital Printing Machine

Prints instantly upon startup and integrates full-line intelligent digital printing and post-press processes, specifically engineered for short-run and quick-turn jobs.

Stripping & Blanking Machine

Efficiently removes edge and hole waste from die-cut items, significantly improving post-processing productivity in packaging production.
