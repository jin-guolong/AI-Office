FAQ
What industries can die cutting machines be used for?

From cigarette, wine, and luxury goods to medical, daily chemicals, food and beverages, and even transportation packaging, die cutting machines add greater value to packaging processing.

What are the common types of foil stamping and die-cutting machines, and how do you pick the right one?

Flatbed combination machine – Perform both foil stamping and die cutting in one operation, ideal for flat materials.

Rotary hot foil stamping die cutter – Designed for high-speed production, commonly used for labels and flexible packaging.

Semi-automatic die cutting machine – Offer a good balance between precision and affordability, suitable for medium-volume runs.

Key factors to consider:

The material you work with (e.g., paper, plastic, or foil-laminated substrates).

The level of detail required (intricate patterns versus basic shapes).

The production speed you need (manual feed versus automatic feed).

The machine's build quality and rigidity to ensure stable, repeatable output.

What types of materials are compatible with flatbed die cutting machines?

Dayuan die cutting machines have a wide application range. Our cardstock series can process cardboard from 80 to 2000 g/m², cardboard thickness from 0.1 to 2 mm, and corrugated board up to 4 mm. Our corrugated board series can process corrugated board up to 9 mm, covering various flute types such as F, E, B, C, A, and AB. In addition, they can also process special materials like thin plastic sheets. For detailed information on applicable paper types, please send us an inquiry.

What is the delivery time for Dayuan die-cutting machines?

Standard models: about 30 days after order confirmation. 

Customized orders: 45–60 days or more, depending on complexity. Accurate schedule provided upon order confirmation. Contact sales for custom quotes.

What is the designed service life of Dayuan die cutting machines?

Under standard operation and regular professional maintenance, the designed service life is 20 years or more. High-grade materials and robust structure ensure long-term durability and stable performance.

Does Dayuan die cutting machine offer customized solutions?

Customization services include machine format, multi-station function selection, feeding and delivery methods. Integration with existing production lines, mirror die cutter combination, or complete production lines is supported based on process requirements. Specific requirements can be submitted to the sales team for a proposal.

What tests and measurements are required during assembly?

Clearance measurement, torque tightening, parallelism/levelness correction, bearing preload, seal gap check, alignment, oil circuit pressure testing, airtightness testing, etc.

What services will Dayuan provide for you?

We provide full lifecycle services for die-cutting machines, including customized solution design, installation and commissioning, operator training, spare parts supply, as well as long-term system optimization and after-sales support.
