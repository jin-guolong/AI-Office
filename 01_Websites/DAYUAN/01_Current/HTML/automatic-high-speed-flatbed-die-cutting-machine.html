Overview

The BHT-1060/1060A series is the optimal solution for Dayuan's Automatic High Speed Flatbed Die Cutting Machine. It is specifically designed for intelligent production that pursues ultimate production capacity and high return on investment. This series adopts the Siemens electrical control system and carbon fiber gripper bars, ensuring the equipment maintains stability and precision during high-speed operation. It supports direct adjustment of die cutting precision via the touchscreen, easily handling die cutting processes with multiple layouts and few connection points. With high reliability, intelligent correction and stable output, it provides customers with efficient and intelligent die cutting solutions.

Paper Feeding Unit

  • Fish-scale powerful feeder head with 4 suction and 6 delivery. The suction head can be adjust to various angles according to paper deformation.
  • Horizontal paper separation blower.
  • Full automatic non-stop alternating operation of main and auxiliary paper stacks without operator intervention. (Available as an option)
  • Mechanical left/right fine-tuning device for the main feed pile.
  • The paper alignment adjustment device can adjust the distance before the paper reaches the gauge without stopping the machine.
  • Blow-suction dual-purpose vacuum pump of German Becker brand.

Paper Conveying Unit

  • Slowdown mechanism for sheet feeding at the front gauge.
  • Ultrasonic double sheet detection system with effortless calibration, precise operation, and protection against surface scratches.
  • Four front gauges can be adjusted individually.
  • Paper pressing device feeds warped sheets smoothly to the front gauge.
  • Push-pull dual-purpose side gauges on both sides.
  • Photoelectric detection on both side and front gauges.
  • If the paper is not in place, the system can force the gripper to open and close.

Die Cutting Unit

  • High-tech carbon fiber gripper bars and positioning structure, fine steel small gripper teeth and supporting gripper plates.
  • Servo controlled gripper bar positioning device: Each gripper bar can be adjusted by PLC touch screen button to ensure the accuracy of gripper bar, with permanent accuracy of ±0.075mm (Invention Patent).
  • Pressure regulating device, which can realize accurate control of pressure through PLC touch screen.
  • The 15mm die-cut steel backing plate and a 5mm combined lower backing plate with +/-0.9mm vertical and horizontal fine-tuning device.
  • Central positioning system.
  • Die-cutting chase automatic push-pull mechanism.
  • Die-cutting chase quick locking device.
  • SIEMENS (INNOMOTICS) brand main motor.

Main Machine(refrigeration) cooling and lubrication mechanism

  • The whole machine adopts a centralized automatic oil supply system to ensure that the transmission parts are not short of oil.
  • Automatic oil pumping circulation cooling device ensures the lubrication of the main machine during long-term high-speed operation and maintains the normal temperature of the lubricating oil of the main machine.

Delivery Unit

  • Roll-up curtain type auxiliary paper delivery device, can realize non-stop paper delivery.
  • Adjustable mechanical paper delivery brush press mechanism to help gripper bar discharge and stack paper.
  • Paper delivery alignment device.
  • Photoelectric detection of upper and lower limit switches can effectively prevent excessive paper pile stacking and paper curling up.
  • The delivery section can be controlled through touch screen.
  • The two-way auxiliary air blowing.

Electrical Unit

  • Germany Siemens PLC control system.
  • The die-cutting unit and the delivery unit adopt 10.4-inch and 7-inch Germany Siemens touch screens.
  • The machine adopts Germany Moeller brand relays, AC contactors, air switches and buttons, to ensure the stability and reliability of electrical parts.
  • The machine adopts Japanese Omron brand photoelectric switch, optical fiber, encoder and sensor, to ensure the accuracy and stability of electrical detection of each part of the action.
