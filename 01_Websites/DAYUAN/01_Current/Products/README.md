# Current Product Records

## Purpose

Store the current DAYUAN product inventory and source materials used to verify product coverage, specifications, models, applications, and page relationships.

## Contents

- Product and model lists
- Current specifications, features, applications, options, and related product records
- Product URL mapping and category assignments
- Source files supplied by the company or captured from the current website

## Rules

- Record the source of every technical claim and mark unconfirmed information for review.
- Keep product names, models, units, and specifications consistent with approved company materials.
- Do not invent performance data, compatibility, certifications, or application claims.
