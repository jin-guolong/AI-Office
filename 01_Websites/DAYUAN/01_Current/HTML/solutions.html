Tobacco Packaging

With the upgrading of consumer tastes, the market has raised multi-dimensional requirements for packaging – to create anti-counterfeiting labels that are hard to replicate and build strong brand recognition. This drives the upgrade of cigarette packaging production toward high-precision, distinctive and mass-producible solutions.

  • Holographic hot stamping anti-counterfeiting technology
  • High-precision hot stamping process
  • Flexible customization
Wine Packaging

With the global consumption upgrade, consumer demand for premium alcohol continues to rise, and the global alcoholic beverage market is expanding steadily. This growth is placing higher demands on packaging processes in terms of precision, appearance, and anti-counterfeiting performance.

  • High-precision hot stamping process
  • Premium deep embossing effects
  • Personalized custom solutions
Luxury Packaging

In the beauty market, competition has extended from the product itself to exterior design, with consumers valuing personalized and user-friendly packaging. As market expectations for brand added value rise, packaging needs to convey brand aesthetics and build brand recognition.

  • Flexible customization
  • Compatibility with diverse paper materials
  • Integrating holographic hot stamping, hot stamping, deep embossing
Medicine Packaging

As medicine management becomes stricter and patient medication more personalized, packaging must integrate anti-counterfeiting and traceability technologies and user-friendly packaging structures to ensure medication safety. This is driving the upgrade of packaging toward high-precision, anti-counterfeiting, flexible and high-efficiency solutions.

  • High-efficiency mass production
  • Holographic hot stamping anti-counterfeiting technology
  • High-precision die cutting and stripping
Daily Chemical Packaging

In the daily chemical market, brand and visual design have become the core of competition, driving packaging toward more exquisite design and higher-precision manufacturing, which in turn creates a demand for high-precision die cutting solutions with efficient mass production capabilities.

  • High-speed continuous production
  • Integration of multiple processes
  • Rapid changeover capability for multiple specifications
Food & Beverage Packaging

With the diversification of consumption scenarios, the food and beverage market has raised comprehensive requirements for packaging safety and convenience. This is driving the upgrade of packaging toward integrated solutions that combine brand stylization with green sustainability.

  • Compatibility with Diverse Paper Materials
  • High-efficiency multi-specification production changeover
  • Reinforcement of box load-bearing structures
Transportation Packaging

As e-commerce becomes increasingly globalized and automated, the logistics industry faces challenges such as longer transportation distances and a growing emphasis on unboxing and delivery experiences. This places demands on packaging for solutions that balance personalized protection with high-efficiency productivity.

  • Compatibility with diverse corrugated boards and cardboards
  • High-efficiency mass production
  • Precise structural forming
