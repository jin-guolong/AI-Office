# Current Website Documents

## Purpose

Store downloadable documents and reference files currently used by the DAYUAN website and its industrial buyers.

## Contents

- Product catalogs, brochures, manuals, datasheets, and technical drawings
- Certificates, company profiles, and other approved downloads
- Source URL, document date, language, and revision details where known

## Rules

- Do not alter approved technical specifications, certifications, or legal statements.
- Mark expired, duplicate, or unverified documents for review without deleting the archive copy.
- Do not store confidential files or documents without confirmed publishing permission.
