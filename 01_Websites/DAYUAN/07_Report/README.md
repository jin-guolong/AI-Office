# Reports

## Purpose

Store client-facing and internal reports for website operations, SEO, content, data, and project progress.

## Store Here

- Monthly SEO and website operations reports
- PowerPoint decks and exported PDFs
- Website audit and optimization reports
- Content, technical, conversion, and project-status reports
- Source workbooks, appendices, review copies, and approved final versions

## Rules

- State client/project, reporting period, comparison basis, and data sources.
- Connect findings to buyer behavior, inquiries, business impact, and next actions.
- Keep draft, review, and final versions clearly named.
- Do not silently overwrite an approved report or expose confidential internal notes.
