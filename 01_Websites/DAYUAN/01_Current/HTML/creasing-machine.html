FAQ
What materials can a creasing machine handle?

The creasing machine can handle common cardboard, corrugated board, plastic sheets, leather products, and other similar materials.

What's the difference between your manual, semi-automatic, and fully automatic models?

Manual machines – A cost-effective choice for low-volume or custom work.

Semi-automatic models – Strike a balance between productivity and operator involvement.

Fully automatic systems – Equipped with auto feeding, stripping, and other advanced features, they are built for high-volume, continuous production, maximizing efficiency while minimizing labor costs.

What is Dayuan's reputation in the market?

Over the past 27 years, we have established a strong reputation based on proven reliability and continuous innovation. With more than 20,000 machines installed worldwide and long‑standing partnerships with over 90 agents, we are recognized as a dependable partner for durable and high‑performance machinery. Client testimonials and machine operation videos are available on our YouTube channel.

What is the delivery time for Dayuan creasing & cutting machines?

Standard models: about 25 days after order confirmation. 

Customized configurations: 40-50 days, depending on complexity. An accurate delivery schedule will be provided upon order confirmation.

What is the designed service life of Dayuan creasing & cutting machines?

With proper operation and regular maintenance, the designed service life of classic Dayuan clawshell die cutting machines can reach 20 years or more. High-quality materials and robust structural design ensure long-term durability and consistent creasing and die cutting performance.

Does Dayuan creasing & cutting machine offer customization services?

Yes. Customization options include fine adjustment device, automatic oil pump, protective fence, air brake, and other optional features. Solutions can be provided based on specific requirements. For customization details, please contact the sales team.

What tests are performed before equipment delivery?

We conduct thorough no-load operation tests, along with accuracy, pressure, speed, safety, and electrical tests. The machine is only shipped after all performance criteria have been met.

Can missing accessories be resent if found after receiving the goods?

Yes. Please provide the packing list photo, overall goods photo, and names of missing items. We will verify and reship.
